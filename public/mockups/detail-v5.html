Detail pane v5 — organized around the decision

Every value here is from the seeded Sherlock/Watson/Harbour test corpus, or synthesized in its flavor where the corpus lacks an op. The folds and tooltips on this page are live — tap them.

test corpus only — safe to commit

S1The pane, reorganized around the decision

The old pane was a property sheet: every fact the system had, grouped by data category, decision buttons first. This one is ordered by the questions a reviewer actually asks, top to bottom: what does this do, what will my vault look like if I keep it, why should I trust it, and only then the decision. The numbers row is gone — chars moved into the consequence zone as a growth line, the refs count became the actual quoted evidence, and confidence became a sentence in the evidence zone.

S2One consequence rendering per operation

The middle zone is op-specific — each operation states its outcome in its own shape instead of sharing one generic stored/proposed box (and the metadata ops no longer fall through to a raw “also proposed” dump). Note references resolve to titles with type icons everywhere, including targets that only exist later in the same batch.

S3Long entries — folding beats scrolling

Your worry, taken seriously: after-state rendering could mean re-reading a whole section to find two new lines. The answer is diff-style context folding — unchanged stored text collapses to a labeled fold row, and the folds here really expand (tap them). The change region always stays visible with one or two context lines around it. Appends make this easy: new lines always land at the end, so there is exactly one fold, at the top.

S4Where the taxonomy words went

static / merge / medium risk assaulted from the middle of the old pane as three floating chips. They are extractor classifications — evidence-zone material. Two demotions, both keeping the dotted-underline education. Either way, the exceptional values (change, rewrite, high risk) already surface as flag sentences in this zone, so demoting the defaults hides nothing that matters.

S5Editing is a mode you enter, not a form that is always armed

The old pane rendered the proposed text inside a live textarea with a disabled Save floating below — everything read as an unfinished form. Now the pane is read-only until the edit button in the consequence zone is pressed.

S6At 390px — same pane, sticky decision in the thumb zone

Nothing forks: the same zones stack, the folds work harder (stored context folds to one line sooner), and the decision bar detaches to stick at the screen bottom — primary actions in the thumb zone, reading above.
