1 · The problem

Primary is an accent fill and secondary is a hairline box on --surface-1; both already exist in the codebase as .dbtn.is-primary and .dbtn. Ghost is the floor — no border, no fill, dim ink — which is what .fold-btn and .toast-action already draw. Tertiary has to be legibly between secondary and ghost, and the only currency left is accent ink and how much chrome carries it.

2 · Candidates, in the ladder

Each row is the full hierarchy with one candidate spliced into third position. Read each row left to right and ask whether the fourth step is still a step down.

T1 accent ink · no chrome

A bare accent label. This is what .toast-action already is.

  • strength Quietest option that still reads as accent, so the step down from secondary is unmistakable.
  • strength Costs no horizontal space — survives the 3-column dock and a dense row.
  • weakness Only one declaration separates it from ghost: ink color. On a busy surface that is a thin distinction.
  • weakness Accent-on-canvas with no border reads as a link, and DESIGN.md §1 already spends accent on links.

T2 accent ink · accent outline · no fill

The primary's color, drawn as outline instead of fill.

  • strength Unambiguously a button, and unambiguously not the primary — one axis (fill vs outline) carries the whole difference.
  • strength Same box geometry as secondary, so siblings align without special-casing.
  • weakness Louder than secondary, not quieter — it inverts the ladder it is supposed to extend.
  • weakness Collides with .zbtn[aria-pressed="true"] and .dbtn2.save-on, which already mean selected, not tertiary.

T3 accent ink · accent outline · accent wash

Byte-identical to today's .dbtn2.save-on.

  • strength Reads as a distinct, deliberate tier at a glance.
  • weakness Louder than both secondary and T2 — it is nearly a second primary.
  • weakness The wash is the established pressed signal across .zbtn, .dbtn2 and .segbtn.is-pos. Spending it on a variant means a pressed tertiary has nothing left to say.

T4 accent ink · neutral outline · no fill

Secondary's box, but the label is accent instead of --text. Note the border is --edge-strong, one step up from secondary's --edge, so the box itself does not disappear against the accent ink.

  • strength Genuinely between secondary and ghost: keeps the box, spends only the ink.
  • strength Leaves the accent fill and the accent wash unspent, so primary and pressed both keep their meaning.
  • weakness Secondary and tertiary differ by ink alone at a distance; in a group of two the tier may not register.
  • note Accent ink means interactive (§1), which every button already is — so the ink is doing emphasis work here, not category work.
