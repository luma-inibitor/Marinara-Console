N2Desktop — scope above, views below

Your call: V3, with the counts moved onto the view tabs as badges and the scope row left to do one job. Scope now sits above the views, which is both the visual hierarchy (scope decides what every view shows) and parity with mobile, where scope is also the first thing under the header.

N3The scope picker — a disclosure, two projections

Your call: the control in the bar opens a disclosure. Desktop gets the three panes; mobile gets the same walk one column at a time. Both carry a search field, and neither autofocuses it — opening the picker should not capture the keyboard from someone who came to click.

All characters behaves exactly like All chats and All branches: it is the first row of its column, selecting it widens that level, and the levels below it stay usable — an All characters scope still lists every chat. There is nothing special about the top level.

No icon for scope itself. Every candidate is already spoken for or means something else: filter is the facet control, target and crosshair read as precision or aiming, focus is a camera idea, and the concentric shapes collide with the decision circles. The word SCOPE is five characters and exact, and the three controls beneath it already carry typed icons — the group's meaning is visible without a glyph standing in for it.

The engine settles it: a draft's fingerprint records scope (chatIds, characterIds) and modes / extractionMode as separate fields, and every source carries its own importMode. Scope is where you are; mode is a property of the things you find there. Putting mode in the cascade implies a fourth level that filters the third, and it does not.

So mode belongs with the list's filter control, beside search on Sources — which is also where upstream puts it, below the scope card rather than inside it. It is on the backlog; this console has no mode filter yet.
