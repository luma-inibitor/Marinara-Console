    vale --no-global --ext=.md < .vale/fixtures/rhetoric.txt

The redirect is required: --ext applies to stdin only, and Vale skips a .txt
path handed to it directly. The extension is .txt so the [*.md] section leaves
this file out of the ordinary pass.
